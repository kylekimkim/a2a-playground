# English Translation

Peter Pan is a boy who never grows up and lives on a mysterious island called Neverland.
One day, he takes Wendy, who lives in London, and her brothers to Neverland.
There, the children have adventures with the Lost Boys and the fairy Tinker Bell, and they come face to face with the pirate Captain Hook.
Peter Pan is free-spirited and brave, but he is not very good at taking responsibility.
Meanwhile, Wendy cares for the children and comes to understand the value of family.
In the end, Wendy and her brothers return home,
and Peter Pan remains in Neverland, continuing to live as a boy forever.
This story conveys the meaning of growing up, responsibility, and the innocence of childhood.
